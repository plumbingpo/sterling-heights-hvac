Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Grand Rapids

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of the year.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
